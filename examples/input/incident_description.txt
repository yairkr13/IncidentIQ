On July 29, 2026 around 14:10 UTC, customers started reporting that checkout was
failing on shop.example.com. Submitting an order returned a generic error message
("Something went wrong, please try again") instead of an order confirmation.

The problem showed up about ten minutes after we rolled out release 2.4.1 to the
checkout-service in production, which went out at 14:00 UTC. Within about ten
minutes the error rate on the /api/checkout endpoint climbed from under 1% to
roughly 42%. A few customers in support tickets mentioned seeing the spinner for
a long time before the error appeared, and at least two people said their card
was charged even though the order showed as failed.

The on-call engineer (Priya Nair) was paged at 14:22 UTC when the error-rate
alert crossed its threshold. As of writing this, the checkout-service has not
been rolled back yet — we're still trying to figure out whether this is a
database issue, a problem with the payment provider, or something in the 2.4.1
release itself.

Nothing else in the deployment calendar changed around the same time other than
2.4.1. Traffic levels were normal for a Wednesday afternoon, so this doesn't
look like a load spike.
